Mix & settings

Every sound is synthesized live from measurements of Caltrain Stadler KISS EMUs, not played from recordings. Horn: one note near F#4 (376 Hz) with its measured harmonics and the small pitch drop at the start of each blast. Bell: the electronic bell's seven partials, struck every 0.4 s. Doors: the 1760 Hz closing tone. Motor: the inverter whine lines as they move with speed.

No sound on an iPhone? Turn off the silent switch and raise the volume. Keep this page in front; the screen stays on while it is open where the phone allows it.
